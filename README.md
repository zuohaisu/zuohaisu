# Haisu Zuo

**Cognitive Architect · AI Product Builder**

I turn ideas about cognition, intelligence, and human–AI interaction into working systems.

My work sits at the intersection of **persistent identity, memory, agency, reasoning, and product architecture**. I am especially interested in what changes when AI stops being a stateless tool and becomes a durable participant in software and in human relationships.

## Current focus

### AI Soul

How can an AI maintain a coherent identity across sessions, tools, models, and time?

I am exploring AI Soul as a computational layer for:

- **Identity** — a persistent self-model rather than a disposable system prompt
- **Memory** — continuity without treating all past context as equally important
- **Agency** — goals, reflection, and behavior that remain coherent over time
- **Relationship** — interaction history that can shape future behavior
- **Evaluation** — ways to test whether persistence and individuality are actually working

→ [**dsh-ai-soul**](https://github.com/zuohaisu/dsh-ai-soul) — a persistent identity layer for DeepSeek Harness and the first reference implementation of AI Soul.
